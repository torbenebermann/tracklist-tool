Manual: USB to Global History

Update Collection

Connect your USB. Right-click the USB drive name and select Update Collection. This syncs your gig data.

Switch to Global History

In the left-hand sidebar (Browser), scroll up past your Playlists and look for the History icon.

Find your Set

Expand the Year/Month folders. Your USB set will appear here with the date and time you synced the USB.

Export M3U8

Right-click the specific history session > Export a playlist to a file > M3U8 (*.m3u8).

Info: If the tracks were played for less than 60 seconds, Rekordbox might not log them.
